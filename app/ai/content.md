# Blog

I'm not a professional blogger or something, but how I use AI for my [blogposts](/blog) is:

1. I read up on whatever is in my head.
2. Sometimes ask people about it.
3. Write the entire post by hand.
4. Review It.
5. ~~Send it to an AI model to check for grammatical and spelling errors (since I'm not native English speaker), improve some weird wordings, and reduce some over explanations to make things concise.~~
6. Then do one last review, and publish.

> [!UPDATE]
> Starting from `November 23, 2026`, I decided NOT to use AI help in any of my blogs, even for spelling and grammatical errors.
